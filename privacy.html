Privacy

Privacy policy of TALUS (ESKARP in the United States) — updated 31 October 2026.

Support · Français

In short: TALUS collects no data. No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no network connection.

Data on your device

  • Your games, their analysis, your statistics, nickname and settings are stored only on your device.
  • They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • They are deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

TALUS requests no access to sensitive data (location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone…). The clipboard is used only when you copy or paste a board code.

Children

TALUS is suitable for all ages and collects no data, including children's data.
